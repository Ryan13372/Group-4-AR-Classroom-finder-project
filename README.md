# AR Classroom Finder

Welcome to our project! This app helps students easily find their way around campus.

## What it does
First-year students often get lost trying to find their classes in big, confusing campus buildings. This app lets you search for a specific room number and gives you easy-to-follow directions so you never have to be late or stressed about getting lost.

## Main Features
* **Quick Room Search:** Just type in your room number to find where it is right away.
* **AR Directions:** See clear, step-by-step arrows on your phone screen that guide you straight down the hallway to your door.
* **Arrival Check:** The app lets you know the exact moment you have reached your destination.

## Why We Built This
Walking around a brand new university can be super overwhelming, especially when your phone GPS stops working indoors. We wanted to build a simple tool that uses your phone camera to point you in the right direction without any complicated maps.
