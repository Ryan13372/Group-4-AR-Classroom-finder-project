# Risks & Constraints
## About
- This section describes possible problems and limitations that may arise in the AR Classroom Finder
## Content
* **Accuracy:** Inaccurate location data may lead students to the wrong class
* **Camera Access:** AR requires user to give access to camera
* **Compatibility:** The application may fail on some devices
* **Building Layout:** Without an accurate mapping, the navigation may be challenging
## Priorities
* Focus on accurate classroom data and compatibility
## Next Steps
* Identify more limitations and possible risks
* Identify ways to reduce errors in navigation
